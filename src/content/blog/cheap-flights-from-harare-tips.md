Flying out of Harare isn't cheap, and pretending otherwise doesn't help anyone. But in our experience booking flights for clients every week, there's a consistent gap between what travellers *pay* and what the same seat *costs* — if you know how to shop for it.

Here are the habits that actually move the number.

## 1. Book 6–10 weeks out for international, 3–6 for regional

This is the sweet spot for most routes out of Harare. Booking six months ahead rarely gets you the lowest fare (airlines haven't priced the route aggressively yet), and booking two weeks out means you're paying for certainty. Set a target window and check within it.

## 2. Midweek is your friend — especially for regional routes

Tuesday, Wednesday, and Saturday departures are consistently cheaper than Friday and Sunday. If you can flex your travel by a day, ask for the fare on three different dates and take the cheapest. A one-day shift on routes like Harare–Johannesburg can easily save 20–30%.

## 3. Compare the whole journey, not the flight number

Here's a trap: the same airline code-share can be sold as three different "flights" at three different prices. Also check whether the cheaper fare includes checked luggage. A fare that's $40 cheaper but charges $60 for a bag is not cheaper. When you get a price from us, it always includes the final number with bags — that's the number that matters.

## 4. Consider the connection, not just the direct

Direct flights from Harare exist to a handful of hubs — Johannesburg, Addis Ababa, Nairobi, Dubai, Doha. For long-haul, the "fastest" routing isn't always the best value. A slightly longer connection through a hub you're transiting anyway can shave hundreds off the fare. Just leave yourself at least 90 minutes (ideally 2+ hours) for transfers.

## 5. Split the booking for groups — carefully

For families or groups, sometimes two separate bookings (adults vs. child fares, or different cabin availability) beat one big booking. Sometimes it's the opposite. The point: "one booking" isn't automatically cheapest. If you're travelling with more than two people, it's worth getting a quote rather than clicking through a booking site yourself.

## 6. Use the airline's error tolerance window — or avoid it

Most airlines let you cancel or change within 24 hours of booking without penalty. That's a safety net, not a strategy. The actual strategy is the reverse: don't book on a Friday afternoon when your plans are still fuzzy, because change fees on most routes out of Harare are brutal once the 24-hour window closes.

## The one rule that beats all tips

Book when you've decided, not when you've found "the perfect price". A good fare on the right dates beats a great fare on the wrong ones, and prices out of Harare rarely trend down the longer you wait. When a fare looks right for your plans, that's your moment.
