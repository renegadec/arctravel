# 📸 Photo Checklist — Arc Travel & Tours

Everything on the site that needs a **real photo** instead of AI/Unsplash placeholders.

## How to contribute photos

1. Drop originals (unedited, highest resolution you have) anywhere on your Mac — e.g. this folder or `public/images/user-photos/`.
2. Name files descriptively: `great-zimbabwe-wall.jpg`, `hwange-elephants-drive.jpg`.
3. Tell Claw which destination/page each batch is for (or leave filenames self-explanatory).
4. Claw handles: cropping, resizing, compression to webp/jpg, and wiring into the pages.

**⚠️ Rights:** only use photos you own or have written permission to use commercially (lodges/hotels/partners: get a quick email OK).
